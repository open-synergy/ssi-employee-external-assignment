# Delete Employee External Assignment Agreement Payment Term

## Pre-Condition

- Record is in **Draft** status.
- The document number is still **/** (has not been assigned yet).

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Career Management > External Assignment > Payment Terms**
   menu.
2. Open the record to delete.
3. Click **Action > Delete**.
4. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Post-Condition

- The record is permanently removed and no longer appears in the list.
