# Reload Payslips — Employee External Assignment Agreement Payment Term

## Pre-Condition

- Record is in **Draft** status (the button is only available while in Draft).
- At least one Employee External Assignment is already loaded, so the **Employees**
  field is not empty (see
  [14-load-external-assignment.md](14-load-external-assignment.html)).

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Career Management > External Assignment > Payment Terms**
   menu.
2. Open the record.
3. Go to the **Payslips** tab.
4. Click the **Payslips** button (refresh icon).

## Post-Condition

- Payslips in **Done** status, dated within the Payment Term's own **Date Start**/**Date
  End**, for the employees in the **Employees** field, and not already linked to another
  Payment Term, are linked and appear in the **Payslips** field.
