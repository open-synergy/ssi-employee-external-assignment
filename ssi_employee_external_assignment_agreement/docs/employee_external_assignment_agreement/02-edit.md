# Edit Employee External Assignment Agreement

## Pre-Condition

- Record is in **Draft** status.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Career Management > External Assignment > Agreements**
   menu.
2. Open the record to edit.
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. Change the required fields as needed (e.g. **Title**, **Partner**, **Date Start**,
   **Date End**).
5. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is updated with the new values.
