# View Payment Terms — Employee External Assignment Agreement

## Pre-Condition

- None. The **Payment Terms** button is available regardless of status.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Career Management > External Assignment > Agreements**
   menu.
2. Open the record.
3. Go to the **Payment Term** tab.
4. Click the **Payment Terms** button.

## Post-Condition

- A list view opens showing the Employee External Assignment Agreement Payment Terms
  filtered to this agreement.
