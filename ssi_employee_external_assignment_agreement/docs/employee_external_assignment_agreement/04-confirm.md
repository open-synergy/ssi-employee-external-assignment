# Confirm Employee External Assignment Agreement

## Pre-Condition

- Record is in **Draft** status.
- Record is not linked to a batch (agreements linked to a batch are driven by the
  batch's own workflow and cannot be confirmed independently).
- User has _Can Confirm_ access right.

## Flow

1. Open the **Human Resource > Career Management > External Assignment > Agreements**
   menu.
2. Open the record to confirm.
3. Click the **Confirm** button.
4. Click **OK** on the confirmation dialog.

## Post-Condition

- Status changes to **Waiting for Approval**.
